No middleman workflow

Traditional paste sharing often involves another app or an extra step just to move a link between devices.

🔗

The usual way

Copy the paste URL, open WhatsApp, Discord, email or another messaging service, send the link, and finally open it on the other device.

⚡

Nearby Transfer

Make the receiving device discoverable, open the paste, choose Nearby Share and select the device. The paste opens on the receiving device without manually moving a URL.

🧑‍💻 Receiver

First, make the device that should receive the paste discoverable.

1

Open PasteDB

Open PasteDB on the device where you want to receive the paste.

2

Open Nearby Transfer

Go to the PasteDB Transfer Page.

3

Become discoverable

On mobile, hold three fingers on the screen for 3 seconds, or press and hold the Be Discoverable button for 3 seconds.

Mobile
4

Desktop or laptop

Press and hold the Be Discoverable button for 3 seconds.

Desktop
5

Wait for the sender

Your device becomes visible to nearby users for approximately 10 seconds.

📤 Sender

Once the receiver is discoverable, sending a paste only takes a few taps.

1

Open a public paste

Open the public PasteDB paste that you want to send.

2

Select Nearby Share

Use the Nearby Share option available on the paste page.

3

Choose the nearby device

Discoverable devices appear in the nearby device list. Select the intended receiver.

4

Done

The paste opens on the receiving device. There is no need to manually copy the URL or send it through a messaging application.

📡 How nearby discovery works

A receiver temporarily makes their device available for nearby discovery.

The receiver becomes discoverable for a short period. This keeps the device from remaining continuously visible when Nearby Transfer is not being used.

⏱️ Temporary discovery

The receiving device is visible for approximately 10 seconds after discovery is enabled.

📍 Location permission

Nearby Transfer uses approximate location information to help discover nearby devices. Both devices should allow location access for the best experience.

🔒 About your location

Nearby Transfer may use approximate location information for nearby device discovery. Location information is used to improve the nearby discovery experience and is not intended to be used as a precise location-sharing feature.

PasteDB vs AirDrop

AirDrop isn't the only way to share with nearby devices. PasteDB takes a different approach, focusing on sharing paste links directly through the web.



AirDrop

Apple's nearby sharing feature for transferring files, photos and other content between compatible Apple devices.

📏 Range Approx. 10 m
📱 Devices Apple devices
📡 Connection Bluetooth + Wi-Fi
🌐 Internet Not required for the transfer
⚠️ Privacy note: AirDrop's receiving settings matter. Leaving AirDrop available to everyone in public places can result in unwanted or spam sharing requests.
📋

PasteDB Nearby Transfer

Send a public PasteDB paste directly to a nearby discoverable device without copying a URL, scanning a QR code, or using a messaging app.

📏 Range Up to ~100 m*
📱 Devices Any device with a modern browser
🌐 Connection Internet connection
📍 Location Optional
⏱️ Discovery Visible for only 10 seconds
🔒 Privacy-focused: A receiver only becomes discoverable for a short 10-second window, reducing the time your device is exposed to nearby discovery.

*Actual PasteDB range depends on the network, location, browser and surrounding conditions. The ~100 m figure should be treated as an approximate maximum rather than a guaranteed distance.
